**Title:** L'ultimo treno per Natale — La Linea 24.

In 1976, on Christmas Eve, a mysterious train called Linea 24 vanished with its driver **Elia Vetro**. The train maintained a connection between ordinary reality and a magical outside-time realm that made Christmas possible; the disappearance damaged/faded Christmas. A mysterious **Controllore** invites the player to board and claims Elia sabotaged the line. Each day yields contradictory evidence. Eventually player learns Elia disconnected the line to stop the Controllore seizing control. On Dec 24 player must discover a **third way** to restore Christmas without empowering the antagonist. Keep reveal gradual; do not telegraph twist early.

**Six provisional chapters:** 1–4 Il treno fantasma; 5–8 Valbruma, la città senza Natale; 9–12 La stazione delle voci; 13–16 Il laboratorio fuori dal tempo; 17–20 La casa di Elia Vetro; 21–24 Il capolinea impossibile. Each chapter may have a different static atmospheric background. No explorable 2D environments.

**Approved story beats:** Day 1 mysterious invitation and ticket dated 2026 but stamped 1976; day 2 ticket apparently forged by Elia; day 3 hidden Vagone 0; day 4 safety circuit hints shutdown prevented transfer of control; day 5 Valbruma storefront cipher; day 6 clockmaker's five clients and unusual watch.
